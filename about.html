Our story

Ajusti started with one gown and a WhatsApp group

In 2019 we were three friends in Surulere buying Ankara from Balogun market and sending fittings back and forth on WhatsApp. Customers kept asking the same thing: can I see it before I commit, and will it actually fit?

So we built Ajusti. Every piece on this site is photographed on a real body or form, priced honestly, and cut by a tailor we pay per garment — not per hour rushed. If you send your measurements, we make it to them. If you are between sizes, we cut custom at no extra charge.

What we stand on

Three things we will not trade away.

Fabric you can trust

Real wax print and hand-dyed adire — no thin imitations. We name the fabric on every listing and tell you when a dye lot varies.

Tailors, not factories

Our pieces come from small workshops in Lagos and Aba. We pay per garment, upfront, and we put their names on the order card.

Fit before fashion

Custom measurement is free. A dress that does not fit is not a bargain, no matter how good the print looks online.

Become an Ajusti tailor

If you run a workshop and can cut clean Ankara, adire or leather goods, we would like to meet you.
